🛡️ Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us at EventOps AI.

1. Introduction

Welcome to EventOps AI, an AI-assisted event management platform. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, information may include:

  • Name and email address provided during registration.
  • Event registration and participation details.
  • Messages, feedback, and support requests you submit.
  • Information needed to maintain account security and platform operation.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Manage user accounts and event registrations.
  • Provide event management features.
  • Respond to feedback and support requests.
  • Improve platform functionality and security.

4. Data Protection

We aim to protect information using appropriate security measures. Access to information should be limited to authorized purposes.

5. Information Sharing

Information should only be shared when necessary to operate the platform, with appropriate authorization, or when required by law.

6. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact the EventOps AI project administrator.
